One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — built only from things a person from 1800 already knows: a library and a post office. Free to enter, $2 across 10 places, a house judge compares the sentences two at a time and the best ranked win.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```
